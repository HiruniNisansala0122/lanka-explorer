About This Project

Lanka Explorer is an individual student project that helps travellers discover Sri Lankan destinations. I am developing it to practise semantic HTML, structured data and accessible forms. The final version combines destination search, live weather, rupee conversions, interactive maps and a saved trip planner, with light and dark themes.
